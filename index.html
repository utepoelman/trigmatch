How to Play

1
Objective: Match all 9 trigonometric expressions with their correct exact mathematical value cards (18 cards total).
2
Memory Mode: Cards are face down. Flip over two cards to check if the expression matches its exact value!
3
Face-Up Mode: All cards are face up. Select a function card and click its corresponding exact value card.
4
Customise Options: Switch between Degrees (°), Radians (rad), and toggle reciprocal functions (cosecant, secant, cotangent).
